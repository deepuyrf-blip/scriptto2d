FAQ

Sawaal-jawaab

Ek story me kitni images banti hain?

Aap choose karte ho — "Target images" (jaise 200–300) ya "Seconds per image" (3–6s). Auto chhodo to AI khud script padh kar pacing se decide karta hai.

Manus credit lagega?

Nahi. Analysis + prompts Sarvam se, images Cloudflare FLUX se. Manus sirf aakhri fallback hai — normally 0 credit.

Kitna time lagta hai?

Background me chalta hai. 20 images ~2–5 min, 200–300 images ~1–2 ghante (Cloudflare free tier daily limit ke hisaab se).

Style match hoga?

Reference style sheet memory me save rehti hai aur har image prompt me feed hoti hai — isliye har frame ek jaisa flat 2D cartoon look leta hai.

Page band kar du to?

Job background thread me chalti rehti hai. Job ID save rakho — baad me "Track" se status dekh sakte ho.
